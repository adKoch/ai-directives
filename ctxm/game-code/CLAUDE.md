## Game: levers and feel
- An **interaction lever** is this game's name for a behaviour (the coding rules): one thing the player does or judges
  in one interaction, such as engaging an enemy or outrunning the horde.
- A request set marks a requirement as **feel** when only playing can judge it, and names its lever.
- At a **play session** (RC acceptance, or earlier where the plan puts one): pause, give the owner each feel
  requirement with what to try, what changed and where its tuning values live; record their notes in
  `ctxm/discussions/`. The notes go to `problem-solver` as a small request.
